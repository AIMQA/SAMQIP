No dates here on purpose. Submission windows and portals change every year — always confirm the current deadline and where to submit with the Society for Acute Medicine before you write against the clock. This tool checks structure and word count only; it doesn't submit anything for you.

For completed QI projects demonstrating objective, measurable improvement of Acute Internal Medicine services — patient safety, pathways, documentation, flow, digital systems, wellbeing or MDT interventions.

Before you write — essential content

Will not be accepted

  • Clinical audits without implementation of change, or repeated measurements with no intervention between them
  • A change was implemented, but post-intervention data hasn't been collected yet
  • Project proposals, business cases, or planned future work
  • Implementation is still ongoing and results aren't available yet
  • Abstracts describing an intended intervention rather than completed improvement work
